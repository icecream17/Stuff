# Rich

> Random thoughts

I think a more useful secondary definition to distinguish between rich people and poor people is that rich people know how to make money.
(Alternatively, it is sufficient to have money and just know how to keep it, but I don't relate).

For example, there was this one rich guy who started a project to make a million dollars from scratch.
Well, a phone with internet everywhere.

He quit due to his father's diagnosis of cancer alongside several severe health crises,
but based on his progress he definitely could have done it within the time limit (one year).

However, his strategy revolved around his expertise in business. This is not really the same situation as someone
without that knowledge.

## Making money is easy (?)

> "Just follow these life guidelines, understand the basic strategy (getting a good job + good money habits), and then
research the details so that you can implement the basics!"

I've heard something like this before. But consider the following trade offer:

Rich person gets:
+ 10% of _increase_ in salary for 10 years

Poor person gets:
+ Knowledge and ability to get rich

Pretty sweet deal for everyone involved. The median salary is 70k.
If it was easy to reach one million dollars, the rich person could improve that poor person's salary to 1M, which is a 930k
increase. So the rich person gets 10% over ten years, equals 100% over one year (assuming the yearly salary stays at 1M),
so the rich person gets 930k, and the poor person gets 9.07M. And multiply that with many other people... very lucrative!

**This is very rare**. Based on what currently exists, only the most motivated people, in certain fields,
with good habits, and who honestly had a good chance of figuring it out anyway succeed,
and the rich people don't actually mind 100 failures if 1 succeeds,
so it doesn't really look like this trade offer is actually a helpful idea.
(I'm not sure to what extent this would succeed if a rich person genuinely tried, though).

(To be fair, a rich person makes money in their field that they work in. So for this to work with
people in general, there must be one rich person for each field, willing to do this.)
