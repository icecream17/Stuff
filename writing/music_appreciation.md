# Musical appreciation

Random things I appreciated in songs, with little research.

## Noise / volume

A song generally has focus on one instrument, typically the vocals, with the rest being "background instruments".

I will describe **noise** or **loudness** and similar terms as a musical and physical loudness, whereas **volume** is specifically for physical loudness. What I mean is that, theoretically, you could amplify a whisper until it has the same volume as a shout,

**Noise** or loudness has two different considerations to appreciate: how loud the main track is, and how loud everything is.

This describes how I appreciate the way that the background music joins and leaves, as a whole or in layers, supporting the music.

### Loudness and space

Loud background music actually gives space for all the emotions and everything that the audience and/or the foreground wants to feel.

Although, the average person may instead feel overwhelmed.

### The effect of another layer

The music; its world expands. What that does is a choice of the layer itself. Usually the beat creates rhythm, and establishes movement and flow. And in general, a world expansion pulls you in, if done right.

### The effect of a layer transition

Basically like a plot event.

Don't be afraid to repeat - it gets the music established or something, and then music seems to build off from there.

### The effect of the background music leaving

When the background leaves the foreground alone, the music becomes more singular.

It can create a bit of suffocation as the foreground captures your entire vision.

OR, if the foreground is now quieter, it can be a bit more calm, or nervous, or grounded, or reflective. Basically the energy level is naturally lower.
